The negotiation process

Words in.
Agreed price out.

A cabbie could charge anything for "a ride across town" — Uber can't, because the distance is measured. Services are prone to odd, unexplained price changes when unregulated. We fix that: A fully monitored negotiation process leads to formal agreements.

01

Connect With a Pro for A Desired Service

Connect with a verified professional for the service you need without random searching. Just put in a few clicks and provide the location for the desired service.

02

Negotiate - We monitor

Discuss the details of your service needs with the pro. We monitor the conversation to ensure a fair and protected negotiation process.

03

Pricing Requires both Parties to Agree

Prices are decided before a pro is dispatched, payment is made and processed through the app in order to avoid any unprecedented price changes.

05

Agreement Finalized

Once both parties agree on the terms, the service is confirmed and the pro is dispatched.

06

Rate Service Quality

When the service is complete, you can rate the pro's performance and provide feedback on your experience.
